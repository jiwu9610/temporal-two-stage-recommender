# Final report — temporal two-stage recommender (all-positive objective)

**Objective.** Coverage of *every* observed positive item interaction a user
goes on to have in the future window (multi-positive; positive = review with
rating >= 3, the closest observable proxy for a purchase in this dataset) — explicitly not next-item prediction.
**Protocol.** Global calendar cutoffs `T0 < T1 < T2 < T3` per category:
train on history before T0 (labels [T0,T1)), select on [T1,T2), read the
test window [T2,T3) once per declared evaluation. Label-frame integrity is
enforced at training time by a bidirectional assertion (every future positive
present in the candidate table must be labelled 1, and every label-1 row must
be in the ground truth); each run logs the check.
**Freeze.** `results/p4_freeze/frozen_config_ap.json` — generated by a
pure-read script from the Stage-B reports under a pre-declared rule
(primary: model-selection Recall@100 macro; guardrail: Recall@10 must not
regress vs the A0 reference; ties broken by Recall@10, then smaller
candidate table), with sha256 + code-commit provenance for every input.

### Reading

- **Books — the one hard win.** The text-embedding content source raises the
  candidate ceiling by 20.6% while the conditional hit rate holds
  (0.314→0.322): the gain is coverage of items the interaction-based sources
  cannot reach, and the ranker is not diluted by the extra candidates.
- **Video_Games — threshold relaxation rejected by the test.** Selection-time
  R@10 (+16.1%) reversed to −8.3% at test; the R@100 gain (+1.8%) is at the
  edge of the noise band. The freeze is one-shot and stands as recorded; for
  deployment we recommend the A0 configuration (a post-test judgement,
  labelled as such).
- Electronics / All_Beauty: no reliable gain. All_Beauty remains a
  popularity-baseline category (93% single-interaction users).

### Availability assumption on the Books result

The content source retrieves over the 2023 metadata catalog; the dataset has
no reliable historical listing time. Diagnostic on the locked-test artifacts:
of the 1,653 Books items hit *only* by the content source, **85.2% have their
first observed interaction after T2** — the interaction data can neither
confirm nor refute that they were recommendable at T2. Books being a
backlist-heavy catalog (publication typically long precedes the first
review), most of the gain plausibly survives, but the claim is stated as:
**+23.7% under the metadata-catalog availability assumption**, not
unconditionally.

## 3. Sequence module (ranking position) — a measured negative

Design: residual head `logit = DCN(x) + α · g(candidate, sequence)`, α
initialised at 0, so the untrained model *is* the DCN baseline exactly
(asserted by a structural test) and the sequence branch can only learn a
correction. Engagement sequence = the user's last 20 pre-cutoff interactions
(item id + category + store + rating as sparse ids; absolute-month and
log-gap position encodings); the item vocabulary is rebuilt per training
spec (selection: pre-T0 history; refit: pre-T1), so it is never frozen
earlier than the data allows.

Three preregistered variants per category (HSTU-style gated attention,
target-aware multi-head pooling with a 2-layer encoder, causal
last-state), each vs the in-run DCN, with the tie band defined by two seeds
of the DCN itself:

- Books: the only variant above the noise band on R@100 (+0.4%) regresses
  R@10 by 9.5% → guardrail reject.
- Electronics: everything inside or below the band.
- Video_Games: all three variants tie on R@100 (band ±0.9%); a consistent
  +4–5% R@10 appears across variants but the same metric pattern failed to
  transfer to test earlier in the project, and the preregistered rule does
  not spend a test read on it.

**No sequence configuration was adopted; no test read was spent.** The same
model family scores in the published range on the standard benchmark
(SASRec R@10 = 0.091 on 5-core Video_Games, leave-one-out, vs a reported
band of 0.070–0.097; see `results/anchor/COMPARISON.md`), so the null result
here is a property of the production protocol and population — median
sequence length is 1–2 events, and the DCN's engineered features already
carry most of what short sequences encode — not of the implementation.

## 4. Calibration

Raw ranker probabilities are inflated by the class-weighted loss
(ECE 0.28–0.38); a prior-shift or Platt correction fitted on the selection
snapshot brings ECE to the 1e-5 range on the test snapshot in all four
categories without moving any ranking metric. AUC/gAUC use the standard
Mann–Whitney tie treatment (tied pairs = 0.5); top-K ranking metrics use a
deterministic conservative tie policy (tied negatives outrank tied
positives) — the two are computed separately.

## 5. Protocol incident (disclosed)

An earlier run of this final stage trained every ranker on **first-positive**
labels while evaluating against the all-positive frame: the candidate
builder wrote labels from the single-positive ground-truth file, and the
trainer's `--label-mode` flag switched only the evaluation side. In a sample
of 3,000 multi-target Books users, 985 future-positive candidate rows
carried only 178 positive labels — 82% of reachable future positive interactions were
being trained as negatives. External review surfaced the defect. Fix:
the builder takes the label frame explicitly (labels, coverage ceilings and
rule-weight tuning all on the same frame), training refuses to start if the
candidate labels disagree with the ground-truth frame, and every stage was
re-run end to end; the invalidated artifacts are quarantined under
`archive/results_invalidated_first_positive/` and are not referenced by any
number in this report. A second locked-test read was declared and spent for
the corrected pipeline.

## 6. Known limitations

Review events, not purchase logs (cross-day repeat interactions: 0.23% of raw
rows in Video_Games, 0.01% in Books — repeat recommendation is out of
scope by construction and seen-item masking costs at most that much
coverage). Three of four categories are user-truncated samples of the
public dumps, so item-side counts are sample-scoped. The executed locked
test re-selected ranker hyperparameters on the selection snapshot (held-out
legitimate; the exact-recipe path `--frozen-ranker` now exists for any
future run). Absolute recall values are low by construction: the protocol
is strict global-time with a multi-positive denominator.
